Refund Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refunds are handled for Get Ripped Or Die Trying orders.

When Refunds Are Issued

Refunds are issued for approved returns that meet our Return Policy, and for orders we cancel, cannot fulfill, or that arrive defective due to our error.

Refund Method

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method used at checkout through Stripe. We do not issue cash refunds.

Refund Timing

  • Once we receive and inspect a returned item, refunds are typically processed within 5–7 business days
  • Your bank or card issuer may take additional time to post the credit to your account

Partial Refunds

If only part of an order is returned, we refund the purchase price of the returned item(s). Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or a defective product.

Return Shipping

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless we sent the wrong item or the product arrived damaged or defective. We recommend using a trackable shipping method for returns.

Canceled Orders

If you need to cancel an order before it ships, email mal@getrippedodt.com as soon as possible. If the order has already shipped, the return process applies instead.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a dispute with your bank. Most issues can be resolved faster by emailing mal@getrippedodt.com.

Contact

Refund questions: mal@getrippedodt.com
