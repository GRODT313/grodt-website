Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Get Ripped Or Die Trying ("GRODT," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use getrippedodt.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and shipping address when you place an order or contact us
  • Message content when you use our contact or email signup forms
  • Order details such as products, sizes, and amounts paid

Payment card details are processed by Stripe. We do not store full credit or debit card numbers on our servers.

How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Send order confirmations, shipping updates, and customer support replies
  • Respond to messages and restock requests
  • Improve our website, products, and customer experience
  • Prevent fraud and protect our business

Cookies and Site Data

Our site may store cart contents in your browser (local storage) so your selections stay available while you shop. Analytics or hosting providers may also use cookies or similar technologies as part of running the site.

Sharing of Information

We share information only as needed to run the business, including with:

  • Stripe, for secure payment processing
  • Shipping carriers, to deliver your order
  • Email or form services that deliver messages you send us
  • Service providers that host or maintain the website

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Retention

We keep order and customer communication records as long as needed for fulfillment, accounting, legal compliance, and dispute resolution.

Your Choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you by emailing mal@getrippedodt.com. You can also unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in those emails or by contacting us.

Children

Our site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to mal@getrippedodt.com. Get Ripped Or Die Trying.
